# Trade Republic

> Trade Republic is a Berlin-based digital bank and Europe's largest savings platform, offering commission-optimized trading of stocks, ETFs, derivatives, and crypto, ETF/stock savings plans, interest on cash, and a Visa card. It holds a full German banking license (Trade Republic Bank GmbH) and operates across European markets. Trade Republic publishes no public developer API, no API documentation, and no first-party SDKs; its mobile/web apps use a private WebSocket-based backend at api.traderepublic.com.
